# Image rights — gallery works (commercial / print-on-demand)

Every gallery work can be printed and **sold commercially**. Each sets a public-domain text into a
public-domain image, and the typogram itself (the setting of the text into the image) is an original work
of typographics-art.org, ours to license and sell.

## Why each layer is free for commercial use

- **Texts** — every text is pre-1929 and/or its author died more than 70 years ago → public domain worldwide
  (KJV Bible 1611, UDHR 1948 [UN, freely reproducible], Whitman, Melville, Lincoln, Pater, Darwin, Wells,
  Nietzsche, Hearn, Douglass, Twain, Poe, Tesla, Herschel).
- **Portrait photographs (19th c.)** — published before 1929 and/or photographer died >70 years ago →
  public domain worldwide.
- **Paintings / woodblock prints (pre-1900)** — the work is public domain by age, and a *faithful
  photographic reproduction of a public-domain 2-D work carries no new copyright*: **US** — *Bridgeman Art
  Library v. Corel* (S.D.N.Y. 1999); **EU** — DSM Directive 2019/790 **Article 14**, in force across the EU
  since 2021. So the scans are free for any use, including commercial.
- **NASA / STScI (space images)** — public domain, free for any use **including commercial**, with two
  conditions we already meet: do **not** use the NASA/ESA insignia, and do **not** imply NASA endorsement.

## Notes

- The images rendered in this repo are faithful reproductions of the works above; their commercial-free
  status rests on the work being public domain (above), not on any one file's provenance. The canonical
  sources are listed so a cleaner or higher-resolution scan can be pulled at any time.
- For a print line we would set the `color: "photo"` motif render per work; some of these scans (e.g. the
  full Met Great Wave print, which includes its title cartouche and paper margins) need a crop to the image
  before they read as the picture at card size — a per-work step, not a rights issue.
- Keep a product-side disclaimer that the works are historical/public-domain and that no institutional or
  governmental endorsement is implied (covers the NASA insignia/endorsement condition).
