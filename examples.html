Whole documents on one page

Each one-pager holds the complete text of a document. The letters are tiny; together they form the title, or a picture above it (the original images are linked as “Motif”). Every page below was read back with MuPDF and compared with its source. A one-pager is larger than its source file; each card shows both sizes.

Share the PDF, not a picture of it: at this size a plain image is grey texture to OCR and vision models. The text lives in the PDF's text layer.
